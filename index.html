What is
GROOVE?

GROOVE is a student-led dance, choreography and media-production project created to make learning choreography and making dance media easier to join — including for beginners and students who simply want to dance casually, create, teach, film, perform or meet people through movement.

MEDIA PROJECT PIPELINE

Dance is only half the file.

Because GROOVE is formally an SDMC media project, media production is one of its central outcomes. A project can move from idea to choreography, rehearsal, performance, creative direction, camera work, post-production and publication.
