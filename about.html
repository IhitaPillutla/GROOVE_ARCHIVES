About

Dance community. Choreography lab. Media-production pipeline. Extremely unnecessary personnel archive.

OFFICIAL CLASSIFICATION

SDMC Student
Media Project.

GROOVE is a student-led project at Mahindra University centred on Dance & Choreography under the School of Digital Media and Communication (SDMC). Its motto is LEARN. CREATE. PERFORM.

It was created as an accessible choreography community — not a space intended only for highly trained dancers, and not a general-participation model built around auditions. Students can learn choreography, dance casually, create and teach choreography, perform, make dance covers and reels, produce dance films, photograph or film dance, edit projects and meet people through dance.

Styles are project-dependent.

Commercial choreography, pop, K-pop, hip-hop, contemporary, Western/pop choreography, member-created choreography and other styles can all fit depending on the project.

Media is central, not decorative.

A GROOVE project may run from concept → choreography → rehearsal → performance → creative direction → videography / photography → editing / post-production → publishing.

How it started.

The initial interest survey unexpectedly received around 147 responses: approximately 84% definitely wanted to join, 76% wanted to perform, 76% wanted to help organise and 70% wanted workshops or choreography-learning sessions. After creating another formal club proved difficult, GROOVE was approved as an SDMC Student Media Project.
