GROOVE's first major open session — and the first real-world test of its choreography-learning, event, media-production and community model.

BACKGROUND // FOUNDING ERA → GROUND ZERO ARC

FIRST CONTACT.

GROOVE began from a simple campus need: more people should be able to casually learn choreography, dance together, film covers and reels, and perform without already needing to be established dancers.

An initial student interest survey received around 147 responses. Creating another formal university club was difficult, so GROOVE was eventually approved by Mahindra University's School of Digital Media and Communication as an SDMC Student Media Project.

Ground Zero became the first major open session. It was successfully held and became GROOVE's first practical test of choreography teaching, audience interest, event organisation, media production, community building and the team itself.
